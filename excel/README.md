# Excel phase (later)

Excel is not required for Phase 1. When available, use the processed CSV outputs and create:
- a PivotTable by customer segment and region;
- a SUMIFS reconciliation of invoice and payment totals;
- XLOOKUP checks for customer IDs;
- conditional formatting for overdue aging;
- a reconciliation sheet comparing Excel totals to SQL/Python outputs.

Record the exact filter rules and reporting date. Do not assume invoice status alone determines balance.
